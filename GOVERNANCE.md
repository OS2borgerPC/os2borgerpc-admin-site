# Governance

OS2BorgerPC is part of OS2's product portfolio and follows OS2's
governance model, with focus on transparency, shared ownership, quality,
and reuse.

The product is governed by two working groups:

- Styregruppe (Steering Group)
- Koordinationsgruppe (Coordination Group)

## Styregruppe

The styregruppe has the overall strategic and managerial responsibility.
It sets direction and priorities, approves major decisions, and ensures
that the product has the required resources and budget.

Main responsibilities:

- Ensure common direction and alignment across participating organizations.
- Approve scope, quality, timeline, and resource priorities.
- Ensure key stakeholders are involved and heard.
- Support long-term sustainability and connection to operations.

## Koordinationsgruppe

The koordinationsgruppe owns day-to-day product coordination and delivery.
It translates needs into actionable priorities and works closely with
suppliers and contributors.

Main responsibilities:

- Collect, coordinate, and prioritize development and maintenance requests.
- Maintain dialogue with suppliers and follow up on deliveries.
- Ensure the product remains stable, secure, and continuously improved.
- Ensure compliance with OS2 governance requirements, including
  "follow-or-explain" when a requirement is not met.

## How We Work

Governance work is based on openness, documented decisions, and shared
responsibility in the community. Group members participate on behalf of
their organizations and contribute to a common public-sector product.
